Enterprise Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you an authorized B2B partner with Cisco, HPE, Lenovo, and Hikvision?

Yes. Stewpot Communications is an officially registered B2B partner for over 21 global OEMs including Cisco, HPE, HPi, Lenovo, Samsung, Sophos, Palo Alto Networks, Aruba, Ruckus, Hikvision, and Axis. All hardware and software licenses come directly with genuine OEM serial numbers, direct factory warranties, and certified deployment engineering.

Can you support multi-location branch deployments across India?

Absolutely. We manage multi-city hardware staging, secure bonded logistics, on-site rack-and-stack, and centralized SD-WAN/firewall provisioning across all major commercial hubs in India, ensuring synchronized go-lives with zero operational disruption.

What ongoing support or AMC do you provide post-deployment?

Stewpot offers tiered Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), 24/7/365 Network Operations Center (NOC) remote monitoring, rapid 2-to-4 hour on-site spare replacement pools, quarterly security audits, and dedicated Principal Technical Account Managers.

How does Stewpot approach Business Consulting?

Unlike traditional pure-hardware resellers, Stewpot conducts in-depth business assessments combining Technology, Operations, Finance, and Marketing. We help you define a 3-5 year roadmap, optimize your TCO, and ensure that every rupee spent on IT translates directly into productivity and risk mitigation.
